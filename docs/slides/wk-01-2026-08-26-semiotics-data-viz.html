The symbolic animal

Cassirer argues that the classic Aristotelian definition of the human being as a rational animal is wrong, or at least incomplete. Instead we should think of ourselves as “symbolic animals,” since ratiocination1 is only one expression of the human instinct to think in symbols.

– Kirsch (2021)
