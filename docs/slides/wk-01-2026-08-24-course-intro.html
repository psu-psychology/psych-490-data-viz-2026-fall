Visualization in psychological science

Your turn…

Where do data come from?

One answer

Data are the outcome of a process of measurement.

Visualizing measurement

Your turn

What do we measure in psychological science?

Measures in \(\Psi\) science

  • Internal states (thoughts, feelings, memories, opinions, decisions, judgments…)
  • Physiological states
  • Individual characteristics
  • Environmental characteristics

How do we measure these things?

  • Interviews, questionnaires
  • Observations
  • Behavioral responses
  • EEG, ERP, EKG, salivary assays, brain imaging, …

Questions

  • Are there common types of data visualizations in psychological science?
  • What information is conveyed?
  • How clearly is it conveyed?
  • Do the figures “tell the story” (See Wk-08 “Storytelling with data”)

More specific questions

  • How are the following depicted?
    • Responses to surveys
